# EcoSort AI — Responsible AI Framework

Following the guidelines for the 1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship in collaboration with IBM SkillsBuild and AICTE, EcoSort AI incorporates Responsible AI by design across six core pillars:

## 1. Fairness & Robustness
- **Varied Conditions**: Evaluated across different camera resolutions, lighting variations, indoor/outdoor backgrounds, and object orientations.
- **Unbiased Material Recognition**: Treats materials equally regardless of brand or packaging language.

## 2. Transparency & Explainability
- **Clear AI Origin**: Prominently informs the user that recommendations are generated by an AI model.
- **Detailed Reasoning**: Every recommendation includes a `reason` breakdown detailing visual cues (e.g., "Clear PET texture and recycling resin code symbol detected").

## 3. Uncertainty & Abstention (Refusal to Hallucinate)
- Instead of forcing a false classification on ambiguous items or mixed rubbish, EcoSort AI triggers the `Unknown/Uncertain` state.
- Highlights practical advice: "Multiple waste materials detected. Please separate the organic food waste from the plastic wrapper before disposal."

## 4. Privacy & Ephemeral Data Processing
- Uploaded and captured photos are processed strictly in-memory during inference.
- Images are never stored in databases, nor used to train external models.
- No facial recognition, geolocation tracking, or biometric analysis is performed.

## 5. Local Rules & Governance Alignment
- Recognizes that waste-management bylaws vary across campuses and municipal corporations.
- Frames recommendations as intelligent guidance while advising users to follow local institutional color-coded bin regulations.

## 6. Human Oversight & Decision Support
- EcoSort AI acts as an educational and behavioral nudging tool, not an infallible automated sorting robot.
- Encourages human verification and sustainable everyday decision-making.
