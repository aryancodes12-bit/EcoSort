# EcoSort AI — Future Scope & Extensions

1. **Municipal & Campus RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**:
   - Ingest campus sustainability rules and municipal circulars (e.g. Swachh Bharat guidelines, European Green Deal sorting codes).
   - Dynamically retrieve exact neighborhood guidelines based on GPS/location.

2. **Multilingual Regional Support**:
   - Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, and other Indian regional languages to maximize accessibility across diverse communities.

3. **Smart-Bin IoT Integration**:
   - Integration with optical sensors and ultrasonic bin-fill telemetry on campus smart bins.

4. **Campus Gamification & Impact Leaderboard**:
   - Student sustainability scoring, reward badges for verified proper segregation, and inter-department clean-stream metrics.
